Welcome to Kūʻono Farm

Nestled on the slopes of Haleakalā in Makawao, Maui, Kūʻono Farm is a small regenerative farm growing specialty flowers, tropical fruit, and culinary herbs.

We believe healthy soil grows healthy communities, and we love sharing the beauty of farming through flowers, workshops, volunteer opportunities, and special events.

What We Offer

Cut Flowers

Seasonal bouquets and specialty flowers grown on our farm.

Fresh Herbs

Culinary herbs harvested fresh for local chefs, markets, and home kitchens.

Tropical Fruit

Seasonal fruit grown using regenerative farming practices.

Floral Design

We create custom floral arrangements, installations, and tablescapes for weddings, businesses, special events, and meaningful gifts.

Private Events

From intimate gatherings to educational workshops, Kūʻono Farm offers a peaceful setting for connecting with the land and one another.
