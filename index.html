Welcome to Kūʻono Farm

Nestled on the slopes of Haleakalā in Makawao, Kūʻono Farm is a small regenerative farm growing specialty flowers, tropical fruit, culinary herbs, and creating meaningful connections with the land.

Whether you're looking for fresh flowers, beautiful floral arrangements, workshops, or simply a place to experience Maui's agricultural community, we're happy you're here.

What We Offer

Cut Flowers

Seasonal bouquets featuring flowers grown right here on the farm.

Tropical Fruit

Fresh seasonal fruit grown using regenerative farming practices.

Fresh Herbs

Culinary herbs available throughout the year.

Floral Design

We create custom floral arrangements, installations, and tablescapes for weddings, businesses, memorials, celebrations, and special occasions.

Private Events

Kūʻono Farm offers an intimate setting for farm dinners, workshops, retreats, celebrations, and educational experiences.
