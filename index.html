Our Story

Kūʻono Farm is a small regenerative farm in Makawao, Maui. We grow specialty flowers, tropical fruit, and culinary herbs while creating opportunities for people to reconnect with the land through education, volunteerism, and shared experiences.

Why We Farm

We believe healthy soil grows healthy communities. Every planting, harvest, and gathering is an opportunity to cultivate beauty, stewardship, and connection.
