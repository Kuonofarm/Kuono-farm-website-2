Our Story

Kūʻono Farm was created with a simple mission: to grow beautiful food and flowers while caring for the land that sustains us.

Located on the slopes of Haleakalā in Makawao, Maui, our farm combines regenerative agriculture, conservation, education, and community. Every season brings new opportunities to learn, volunteer, and experience agriculture firsthand.

Meet the Team

Jessica Talbot

Founder • Farmer • Farm Manager

Jessica founded Kūʻono Farm with a vision of creating a regenerative farm that nourishes both the land and the community. She oversees every aspect of the farm, from crop planning and flower production to floral design, grant management, workshops, and long-term farm development.

Her passion is creating a place where agriculture, education, and community come together while demonstrating that healthy land can also build healthy communities.

Deniz Bicakci

Operations & Farm Support

Deniz is the farm's all-around handyman and problem solver. Whether it's repairing equipment, building infrastructure, fabricating solutions, or tackling unexpected projects, he's the person who keeps everything running.

If something needs to be built, tinkered, improved, or figured out, Deniz is usually already working on it.

Lilo

Farm Dog & Official Greeter

Every farm needs a loyal companion, and Lilo proudly fills that role. She welcomes visitors, supervises projects, accompanies volunteers, and never misses an opportunity to follow the crew around the property.

Whether she's keeping watch over the flower fields or asking for a scratch behind the ears, Lilo is an important part of everyday life at Kūʻono Farm.
